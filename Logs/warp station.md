The place where the island is warped from


The warp station os the place where the Halfuaans had a meeting with all branches of the Deneir project. This meeting was to discuss a the next step after discovering that the Deneir project required more energy than anticipated and it would not be charged in their lifetime. 

Catastrophically, during the meeting, an atropal showed up in the warp station. Having all Halruaans who knew of the Deneir project die, wiped out all knowledge of it. 

While fishing in the pond in front of his house [[Harlon Brey]] fishes up deathly fish. The fish are a sickly white and do not struggle or flop around when being caught. They also taste like shit.

The entrance to the warp station is situated under the pond in front of the meatbox. To get into the warp station the Players must find the crack in the rocky bottom of the pond. There they must find a way into the flooded Cave system that connects the pond to the warp station

When there they walk through the abandoned hallways and encounter a dark energy that has the following properties 

When walking through it they receive 2 Necrotic damage per round which rises by 2 every round. Their vision reduces by 5 feet per round. They have disadvantage on Constitution saves at the start of the 2/3 round. They have the bone chilled effect. To not get damaged by this, they must receive a necrotic ward from [[Mamma Yaga]].

When they enter the main room they see an atropal that is connected to a large machine in the center of the room by an umbilical cord.

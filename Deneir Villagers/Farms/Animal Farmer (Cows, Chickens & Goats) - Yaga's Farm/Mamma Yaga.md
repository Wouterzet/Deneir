GG WP :)Mamma Yaga, Human (She/Her) Age: ??? old
Is secretly a benevolent witch and is teaching [[Ögirith]] her art.
Can give the players a necrotic ward

Her house is completely filled with Dreamcatchers, crystals and artifacts. 

When meeting the players for the first time she will say something that indicates she has been watching them.

If meeting her last she will talk about it hurting her feelings that they met with her last and if they are scared of her.

If meeting her first she will talk about how the players must have been in a real hurry to b-line for her house. She will also asks if her and the players know each other. 

She can help the players with the dark energy in the [[warp station]]. She will craft them necrotic wards in the form of small totems. However to craft these items the players must bring her something containing that dark energy (for example a fish from [[Harlon Brey]]'s pond). If they have a fish she will lay it on a wooden table near the wall. She will grab a small stool and violently flatten the fish with it. The fish guts scatter around and hit some of the players in the face. She will then scoop up some of the fish and put it in a mortar and pestle together with some dried herbs hanging from the ceiling and some sparkly dust from a bag. She smears the paste over some small totems which she hands over to the party members.
